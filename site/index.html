Get started in three steps.

The installer detects the AI tools on your machine and writes each one's native format. It backs up anything it would overwrite, and uninstall.sh removes exactly what it added.

1

Install

Every detected workspace, or pick one with --target.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/VenkataAnilKumar/FoundryAI/master/install.sh | bash
2

Describe your startup

Copy a template into your repo as AGENTS.md and fill in your startup type and stage.

cp templates/AGENTS-voice-ai.md ./AGENTS.md
3

Ask

Use a routing skill, or name a specialist directly.

/cofounder "plan our seed raise"

Questions

Does it send my code anywhere?

No. FoundryAI is prompt files. Your AI workspace runs them with the model and data settings you already use.

What does it cost?

Nothing. It's MIT licensed. You pay your AI tool or model provider as usual.

What if my tool can't run subagents?

It still works. Coordinators name the right specialist and take on that role when you ask them to.

Is it only for AI startups?

It's tuned for them, but most of the engineering, product and growth specialists suit any software team.

Hire the team. It costs nothing.
